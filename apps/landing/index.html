R&D

cal-lowrisk is in active development. We're building the backend, mobile app, and admin tooling now. This page is a preview of the product and the team behind it — not a live app (yet).

One question, answered clearly.

cal-lowrisk combines a calorie tracker and a workout tracker into a single, goal-aware picture of your day, week, and month.

Balance at a glance

Calories in vs. out vs. your personalized target — one hero number, with a clear deficit / balanced / surplus status.

Macros that make sense

Protein, carbs, and fat broken out and color-coded, so you see what made up the day, not just the total.

A target built for you

We compute your daily goal from your body data (Mifflin-St Jeor → TDEE), then adjust for lose / maintain / gain.

Local-first food

A catalog oriented toward Indonesian meals and household serving sizes — logging feels native, not translated.

Three steps. That's the whole loop.

  1. 1

    Set your goal

    Pick lose, maintain, or gain. Add weight, height, age, sex, and activity — we do the math.

  2. 2

    Log meals & workouts

    A few taps per entry. Catalog foods, custom foods, and workouts with calorie-burn estimates.

  3. 3

    See your balance

    Daily, weekly, and monthly — net calories against your target, with the macro story behind it.

Callium

Callium is a small team building practical, privacy-respecting health tools. cal-lowrisk is our first product — a modern, enterprise-grade stack built end to end: Go backend, Flutter app, Next.js admin, on PostgreSQL.

  • ◆ Privacy by design — your logs are yours; admins only ever see aggregates.
  • ◆ Built in the open, documented as we go.
  • ◆ Not medical advice — a self-tracking tool, by design.
