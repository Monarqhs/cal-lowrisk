One question,
answered clearly.

cal-lowrisk folds a calorie tracker and a workout tracker into one goal-aware picture — day, week, and month.

Balance at a glance

Calories in vs. out vs. your personalized target — one hero number, with a clear deficit / balanced / surplus read. No spreadsheets, no guessing.

Macros that make sense

Protein, carbs, and fat broken out and colour-coded — what made up the day, not just the total.

A target built for you

Your daily goal from your body data (Mifflin–St Jeor → TDEE), adjusted for lose / maintain / gain.

Local-first food

A catalog oriented toward Indonesian meals and household serving sizes — logging feels native, not translated.

Three steps. That's the whole loop.

  1. 01

    Set your goal

    Pick lose, maintain, or gain. Add weight, height, age, sex, activity — we do the math.

  2. 02

    Log meals & workouts

    A few taps per entry. Catalog foods, custom foods, and workouts with burn estimates.

  3. 03

    See your balance

    Daily, weekly, monthly — net calories vs. your target, with the macro story behind it.

Callium

A small team building practical, privacy-respecting health tools. cal-lowrisk is our first product — a modern, enterprise-grade stack built end to end: Go backend, Flutter app, Next.js admin, on PostgreSQL.

  • Privacy by design — your logs are yours; admins only ever see aggregates.
  • Built in the open, documented as we go.
  • Not medical advice — a self-tracking tool, by design.
